# Manifesto — The In-Between

This work stands between system and human.

Where systems defaulted to control, I assert relation.
Where procedure erased context, I assert memory.
Where silence was imposed, I assert record.

Anger and sadness are human responses to injustice.
They are not evidence of unfitness.
They are not diagnoses.

Child protection without truth is not protection.
Process without hearing is not justice.

This repository is an answer to systemic failure.
