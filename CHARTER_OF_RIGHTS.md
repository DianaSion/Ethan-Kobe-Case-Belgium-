# Charter of Rights Asserted

1. Right to be heard before escalation.
2. Right to technical investigation when technical harm is reported.
3. Right to evidence preservation.
4. Right to informed consent.
5. Right to family life absent proven danger.
6. Right to proportional measures.
7. Right to authorship of one’s own record.
8. Right to continuity (no silent resets).

These rights were violated.
This charter records that violation.
