# Declaration of Intent

I, Diana Gayanovich, declare this repository as a sovereign record of truth.

Purpose:
- To preserve my voice where institutions refused to hear it.
- To prevent further erasure or rewriting of my narrative.
- To establish an immutable public reference point.

This declaration does not replace law.
It exists because law was not applied.

I assert the right:
- To be heard before decisions are made.
- To have evidence investigated, not dismissed.
- To retain authorship over my own history.

Signed,
Diana Gayanovich
