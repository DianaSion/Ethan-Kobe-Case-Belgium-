# Case Overview — Belgium Systemic Failure

## Background and Context

This documentation exists as a public witness record of a case involving child separation following ignored technical complaints in Belgium. The record is maintained to preserve factual, verifiable information about dates, decisions, and the reasons authorities provided.

## Why This Record Exists

This record was created because:

1. **Technical Complaints Were Ignored**: Technical assistance requests and complaints were never properly investigated or addressed
2. **Right to Be Heard Was Denied**: Fundamental procedural rights were not respected throughout the process
3. **Consent Was Misapplied**: Temporary consent provided under specific circumstances was converted into permanent separation
4. **Context Was Omitted**: Decisions and official documents omit crucial technical context that led to the situation

## What This Record Documents

### Systemic Failures
- Breakdown in due process and procedural safeguards
- Failure to investigate technical complaints
- Lack of interdisciplinary examination despite requests
- Absence of proper chain of custody for evidence

### Procedural Violations
- Denial of the right to be heard
- Misrepresentation of temporary consent as permanent
- Failure to provide reasoned decisions
- Omission of technical context in official determinations

### Retaliatory Actions
- Actions taken following legitimate requests for technical assistance
- Consequences for raising concerns about systemic issues
- Pattern of responses to attempts to exercise legal rights
